# 月面拾光 · 月海远航

Player promise: pilot a crafted exploration vessel through a tangible lunar expedition, then explore a miniature moon ruin with your past self.
Feeling: quiet wonder with clear readable danger; calm deliberation in puzzles.
Flight core loop: move and boost to collect starlight and complete visible side objectives while telegraphed moving hazards threaten hull; successful collection increases score, damage consumes hull, failure permits immediate retry. Choice of run/world/ship, moon state and optional route contracts stays intact. Over minutes, waves, bosses and port upgrades change decisions. Good players read trajectories and time boost/phase changes.
Puzzle core loop: choose a direction/role or record a route to reach linked goals while walls, powered doors, boxes and consumed trail constrain plans; completion unlocks recorded progress and another selectable level, mistakes are undone or replayed. Echo advances only on deliberate actions.

Level plan: preserve all authored 30 maps and flight schedules. Orthographic camera with tangible model heights gives depth without confusing existing collision footprints. Floor cell rims and ground markers identify legal destinations; walls are low enough to see behind them. Main traveler white/gold, shadow dark/lilac with distinct crescent silhouette. Gates lift mechanically when powered, lamps inset in floor indicate role, moon cores sit in articulated holders. Ship, threats, objective landmarks must remain visible on narrow screens.

Interaction changes: prior to actual run start, skippable briefing explains mode objective, controls, threat silhouettes and avoidance; no simulation while explaining. Endless waves never reopen briefings. All explanations can be reopened. Keyboard, touch, pointer cancellation, safe-area layout and source-of-truth state are preserved.

Non-goals: redesigning level solutions, adding paid accounts, changing scoring, resetting saves, replacing controls with a new physics genre, or pretending a textured plane is 3D.
